# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in RS2.

**This project is currently released as-is.** It is published for transparency
and to gauge interest; it is pre-1.0 and **not actively seeking external code
contributions** at this stage. There is no support guarantee, and issues/PRs may
not receive a timely response.

## Bug reports and ideas

Issues describing bugs or use cases are welcome and useful, even though I may not
act on them quickly. Please don't expect a roadmap commitment.

## Why PRs aren't being merged yet

RS2 is [dual-licensed](LICENSING.md) (AGPL-3.0 plus a commercial license). To
keep the commercial-license option open, the project must be able to relicense
all of its code — which means any merged contribution would require a Contributor
License Agreement (CLA) granting that right. Rather than impose CLA overhead
prematurely, external contributions are simply not being accepted for now.

If you have something substantial you'd like to contribute, open an issue to
discuss it first.
